# Report Editing Rules

1. **Existing content:** Before removing or replacing report material, put the
   proposed change in `content.md` for the user to inspect. Do not make the
   removal or replacement in the report until the user approves it. Additions
   may be made directly.
2. **Figures and diagrams:** Do not create report figures, graphs, or diagrams
   with TikZ or other LaTeX drawing code. Use external image assets instead.
   LaTeX tables are allowed.
3. **Grayscale style:** Newly created images, graphs, and diagrams should use
   only black, white, and neutral gray.
4. **Figures from papers:** Use source figures and graphs as provided. Do not
   redraw, recolor, crop, or otherwise alter them; preserve their aspect ratio.
   Their original colors are an exception to the grayscale rule. Caption each
   figure with its source citation and verify any required reuse permission
   or license.
5. **Numbers and equations:** Include numerical values and equations when they
   support a specific claim, comparison, or explanation. Cite reported values,
   state relevant experimental context, and do not present published results
   as project measurements.
